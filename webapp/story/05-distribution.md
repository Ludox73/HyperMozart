The stripes correspond to homotopy classes of arcs. Any two stripes are separated by vectors which never hit another curve. 

Something you can see is that hits which where the angle is near $0$ or $\pi$ are less common than others. Ultimately, this is controlled by <i>SantalOOOOOO's formula</i>, which asserts that the likelyhood of an angle $\theta$ is given by $\vert\sin(\theta)\vert$. You can see below the distribution we obtained numerically: the blue curve is the cumulative distribution function and its derivative evidently resembles $\vert\sin\vert$.
