Here you can generate melodies starting with two different marimbas $(X, \Gamma)$. Every time the button to generate the melody is pressed, a new random vector is selected (this is why the corresponding score changes). 

In the default configuration, the density of brown notes is much higher in Marimba B than in Marimba A. This is independent of the randomly chosen vector. This tells us that generic melodies distinguish these two marimbas.
