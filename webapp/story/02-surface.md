In this visualization we only consider surfaces of genus 2. These are described in terms of Fenchel-Nielsen coordinates, which can be adjusted below (for computational reasons, the length of the curves are constrained in some limits).

The multicurve $\Gamma$ can be selected by choosing the pair-of-pants decomposition and the subcollection of active curves. Notice that all possible topological configurations can be obtained in this way.

----
(This second part goes after picture before MaxLength)

After choosing $(X, \Gamma)$, pressing _Run geodesic and save musical data_ will select a random initial vector and calculate the sequence of hits for the given length.

In the two subpanels below, one can visualize the geodesic in a hexagon decomposition corresponding to the given pants decomposition, and listen to the produced melody.  

---
(In the Listen to music subpanel)

Here you see the _score_ of the melody: each vertical segment represent one intersection of the geodesic with the curve of the corresponding color. You can modify the length of the score that is being visualized and select the speed at which the music is reproduced. You can also modify the starting time if you wish.
