We show that if two marimbas $(X,\Gamma)$ and $(X',\Gamma')$ have the same frequencies for every motif--we say that they are _isomelodic_--then for generic vectors $v\in T^1X$ there are vectors $v'\in T^1X'$ such that the corresponding melodies are identical: exactly the same notes played at the same times.

<b>Theorem 1.1</b> 
<i>Let $(X,\Gamma)$ and $(X',\Gamma')$ be two hyperbolic marimbas for which we have a label preserving bijection $\Gamma\simeq\Gamma'$. If $(X,\Gamma)$ and $(X',\Gamma')$ are isomelodic, then there is a full measure set $U\subset T^1X$ and an absolutely continuous map
$$\Phi:U\to T^1X'$$
such that for all $v\in U$ the $\Gamma$-melody of the geodesic in $X$ corresponding to $v$ is exactly the same one that the $\Gamma'$-melody of the geodesic in $X'$ corresponding to $\Phi(v)$.</i>
